Extension & Outreach

Our Programs

Powerful Tools for Caregivers

  • A six-week self-care program for family and informal caregivers of adults with chronic conditions.
  • Practical skills in stress management, communication, and problem-solving.
  • Peer support and resources to strengthen confidence and well-being.

Senior Planet

  • Hands-on technology workshops for older adults across Alabama.
  • Practice finding reliable health information, accessing online resources, connecting with others, and staying safe online.
  • Build digital skills and confidence for more connected and independent aging.
